ICICI Bank Relationship Manager Feedback Engine

An enterprise-grade, data-driven platform designed to collect authentic customer feedback, calculate Net Promoter Scores (NPS) in real-time, and track service resolution performance.

1. Controlled Distribution

Unique, tamper-proof single-use links (`/s/[token]`) dispatched via SMS/Email to eliminate duplicate responses and guarantee authentic data collection.

2. 3-Step Frictionless UI

Mobile-first 60-second survey flow covering NPS (0-10), CES Resolution Ease, and 8 RM Evaluation Aspects with dynamic validation.

3. Live Executive Analytics

Real-time NPS engine (+100 to -100), sentiment classification, funnel conversion rates, and plain-English self-explaining tooltips.

Slide 2

System Architecture & Stakeholder Roles

Administrators & Management

Operations Team
  • Generate token batches (+1, +10, +100, custom) for dispatches.
  • Download CSV lists of active survey links.
  • Monitor live NPS, Open Rates, and Completion Rates.

ICICI Bank Customers

Survey Recipients
  • Receive secure, non-transferable SMS/Email survey link.
  • Complete guided 3-screen survey in ~45 seconds.
  • Provide optional qualitative feedback with minimum 20-character rule.

Automated Engine (Supabase)

Backend Core
  • Auto-transitions status: `Pending` → `Opened` → `Completed`.
  • Computes real-time NPS formula: `% Promoters - % Detractors`.
  • Stores JSON payloads for instant CSV/JSON exports.

Business Value & Compliance Highlights

100% Data Integrity & Anti-Spam

Token status auto-invalidates upon submission (`completed`). Customers cannot submit multiple responses, ensuring accurate executive reports.

Immediate RM Performance Coaching

Low NPS scores or `Difficult` resolutions are flagged instantly in the Responses Explorer for quick follow-up and relationship management.

Instant CSV & JSON Exporting

Allows management to export raw datasets at any time for custom offline auditing, PowerBI integration, or CRM sync.

Self-Explaining Metric Cards

Every metric includes mouse-over tooltips written in simple English, enabling team members to understand formulas without SQL knowledge.
